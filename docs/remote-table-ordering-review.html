Light POS · Implementation review

Remote table ordering

One controlled flow from an expiring table QR to cashier-approved POS items.

1 · Session QR

Implemented

One active session per table, printable QR and share link, operator/time/table binding, expiry, and tenant default duration from 5 minutes to 24 hours.

2 · Customer order

Implemented

Guest-only mobile menu. No POS shell or authentication. Duplicate taps reuse the pending request. Submissions wait for cashier approval and do not reserve stock.

3 · Cashier approval

Implemented

Queue with approve/reject. Rejection requires a reason. Approval revalidates menu price and availability, then transactionally creates or appends to the table's held order.

Safety and verification

  • Tenant/location scope enforced by authenticated staff endpoints.
  • CSRF required for staff mutations; public payloads validated and bounded.
  • Concurrent repeat approval fails closed after first resolution.
  • 31 backend tests and 136 frontend tests pass.
  • Typecheck, lint (zero errors), and production build pass.
